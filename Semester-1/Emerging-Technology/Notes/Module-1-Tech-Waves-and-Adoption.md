# Module 1 — Tech Waves & Adoption Frameworks

## 1. What is an Emerging Technology?
An emerging technology is a new or rapidly developing technology that may significantly change how people live, work, communicate, or solve problems.

**Examples:** Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, Extended Reality (XR), and Quantum Computing.

## 2. Technology Convergence
Technology convergence happens when two or more technologies work together to create new capabilities.

**Example:** A smart watch combines sensors, software, wireless connectivity, and data analysis.

## 3. Technology Adoption
Technology adoption is the process through which people or organizations begin using a technology. Factors include cost, usefulness, ease of use, trust, skills, infrastructure, and risk.

## 4. Hype Cycle
A hype cycle describes how expectations about a new technology may change over time. Common stages:
1. Innovation trigger
2. Peak of inflated expectations
3. Trough of disillusionment
4. Slope of enlightenment
5. Plateau of productivity

## 5. Technology Readiness Level (TRL)
TRL is a scale used to describe how mature a technology is, from early research to a system proven in real-world operation.

## 6. Use Case vs Capability
- **Capability:** What a technology can do.
- **Use case:** A specific situation in which that capability solves a problem.

**Example:** Image recognition is a capability; identifying damaged products in a factory is a use case.

## Quick revision
- Emerging technology: new or rapidly developing technology with potential impact.
- Convergence: multiple technologies working together.
- Adoption: beginning to use a technology.
- TRL: technology maturity scale.
- Use case: practical application for a particular problem.
